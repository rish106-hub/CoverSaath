# Knowvia identity

## Tagline

**Know your cover. Before you need it.**

It is plain, relevant to an insurance policy, and does not imply claim approval or insurer control.

## Mark

The K is made from a route: a stable vertical line, a clear branch, and a gold destination marker. It gives `know` a visual form, while `via` is the route to the next action. The mark retains the established Deep Tide, Harbour, Signal Gold, and Mist palette.

For LinkedIn, use `knowvia-linkedin-logo.png` as the profile image. Use the wordmark only where there is enough horizontal space.
