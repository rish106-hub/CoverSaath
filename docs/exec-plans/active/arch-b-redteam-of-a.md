## Part 1. Lapses in arch-a (journey, chat, voice, response)

| # | Sev | Lapse | Why it matters | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | H | **Analytics leaks health answers.** Section 6 emits `question_answered` with the option id. For `ped.has_any` the option id *is* the health declaration ("yes"). | Health data would leave the household to PostHog under the `analytics` purpose, which was never consented for health data (ADR 0033 D1: no free text, but an option id of a health question is just as revealing). | Catalogue items get `sensitivity: health | money | none`. For `health` and `money` items the event carries only `answered: true` and the question id, never the option. A test fails if a health item's option reaches `analytics_outbox`. |
| B2 | H | **Streaming before validation.** Section 3 renders the headline "from `meta` + first `delta`" while 4.1 says drafts that fail are dropped and "never shows a half answer". Both cannot be true. | A headline like "Covered" on screen for 800 ms, then replaced by "documents don't say", is exactly the false reliance G1 forbids. | The model call is not token-streamed anyway (ADR 0031 D7). Run 4.1 on the complete draft first, then stream the *validated* card. The streaming is only the UI effect. The `meta` event shows "Checking your policy..." until then. |
| B3 | H | **Gnani gets raw health speech before any gate.** The privacy gate (ADR 0030) redacts text; audio cannot be redacted. "My mother's chemo" goes to Gnani as audio. | Gnani becomes a processor of health data. That needs its own row in `processor-register.md`, a recipient in the `voice_intake` consent scope and the notice ("your voice is sent to Gnani to be turned into text"), plus data-residency confirmation (India). | Add Gnani to the processor register and the purpose registry (`recipients: gnani`, `modelEligible: no`). The voice consent sheet names it. No STT call without that exact grant (re-checked like the model gateway). FOR LEGAL REVIEW. |
| B4 | H | **"I'm in hospital now" collects health data before consent.** Screen 1 lets a signed-out user jump to screen 9, which asks who is admitted, which city, planned or emergency. | That is health data at the most stressful moment, with no consent sheet shown. | The signed-out emergency card shows only data that is not personal: the insurer helpline from FSS, the cashless steps and the documents list. The "who / where" chips appear only after the one-tap emergency consent (short form, FOR LEGAL REVIEW), and nothing is stored before it. |
| B5 | H | **The `covered` verdict with unverified inputs.** R1 lets `covered` through whenever a `ruleResultId` exists. A rule computed over `extracted_unverified` own-PDF values (for example, an OCR'd policy start date) gives a confident "covered" from an unchecked number. | False reliance on a misread date. | Verdict `covered` / `not_covered` only if every fact the rule used is `user_confirmed` or `reviewed_reference`. Otherwise the verdict is `depends`, with an action "Is this right? [value]" for the unverified input. Lift happens on one tap. |
| B6 | M | **The waiting-period countdown ignores portability and continuity.** "Knee surgery covered from 12 Mar 2027" assumes the policy start date is the waiting start. Ported or renewed-from-another-insurer policies get credit for prior years (IRDAI portability). | A wrong date, shown as a precise countdown. | Add a catalogue question `portability.prior_cover_years` (date presets) as a required input of the waiting-period rule. Until answered, the tile shows "depends: did you move from another insurer?". |
| B7 | M | **TTS cache (your Q4): yes, headlines carry personal data.** "Covered after 2 years, up to Rs 50,000" plus an end date is derived from the person's policy. | A global cache keyed by text hash stores personal audio once per unique text and lets timing reveal that someone had this answer. | A global cache only for template strings with no numbers, dates or names (refusals, "documents don't say", catalogue question prompts). Personal headlines are synthesised fresh and not cached. **Bonus:** pre-render TTS for every catalogue prompt in en and hi at build time. It is free at run time, and question read-out helps low-literacy users most. |
| B8 | M | **Feedback "Wrong" is a dead end.** Thumbs down plus "Wrong" stores a reason, but the user often knows the right value ("my sum insured is 10 lakh, not 5"). | That is the most valuable correction we can get, and it is lost. | When a wrong answer cites an own-PDF fact, offer "What's the right value?" with presets. The tap writes a `user_declaration` (conflict resolution, ADR 0023) and the card re-renders. The feedback row links to the correction. |
| B9 | M | **Five questions before the card (your Q1).** Value comes after five taps of questions. | Drop-off risk. The owner's rule is minimum effort. | Show the card first, with "Not checked yet" badges. Ask at most 2 questions before it, and only ones that change a tile on the card (members, PED). The rest go in the "improve your card" strip. Measure drop-off at each question (events, not content). |
| B10 | M | **Planner uses tile opens (your Q2).** | Fine inside the same purpose (serving this user) if it is computed at request time and not stored as a profile. | Do not persist "interest" scores. Compute them from this session's events. State it in the purpose registry. |
| B11 | M | **Member names on screen 4 (your Q3).** | The user's own document, shown to its owner: low risk. Risk rises when member invites exist (R3). | Show them as is for v1 (owner only). Before invites, show first name + relationship, and gate per-member visibility (ties to R3). |
| B12 | M | **Number check (your Q5).** Computed numbers (730 days = "2 years", end date) would be dropped. | Correct answers dropped means more "unknown" answers, and users lose trust. | Each rule result emits every rendering it allows (`days: 730`, `years: 2`, `endsOn`, `amountMinor`, Indian-format strings). The validator accepts a number if it equals any rendering of a cited fact or a rule output. That is one shared normaliser (R7). I'll supply real number formats from the 4,322 insurer PDFs (lakh, crore, "Rs.", "₹", "INR", "% of SI", "per day") as golden cases. |
| B13 | L | **Languages.** en and hi only. | Gnani covers more Indian languages, and many users prefer Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali or Kannada. | Keep the catalogue keyed by locale from day one (`prompt[locale]`). Ship en and hi, and add others when a human translator plus legal review exists (your Q6: agreed, no machine-translated consent). |
| B14 | L | **Helpline numbers come from FSS but aren't collected.** The unknown card's call button needs the insurer's claims/helpline numbers. | The collected PDFs (claim forms, CIS) contain them. | Squad B adds `insurer_contacts` extraction (deterministic regex over the text layer, page-cited, `extracted_unverified`) to the free pipeline. |

Best parts I'd keep untouched: the question catalogue with fixed reviewed copy (rule 6); the card as the only reply shape; drop-to-unknown with a next step; push-to-talk plus confirm-before-send; the 25-word spoken headline; and status badges in place of disclaimers.
