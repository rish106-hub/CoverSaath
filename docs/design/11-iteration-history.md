# Knowvia landing and onboarding design iteration history

Updated 13 October 2026. This is historical design work, not implementation or a current release status.

The CP0 to CP7 work produced a Claude Design canvas with 14 artboards covering desktop and mobile landing pages, onboarding O1 to O7, extra states, foundations, components and motion. Sources are mirrored in `docs/design/claude-design/`; the brief, research, tokens and review are in `docs/design/00` through `08` and `cp1/`.

The design review recorded seven High, 16 Medium and 18 Low findings. The then-current notes report the High findings fixed in the design; see `08-review.md` for the resolution table. Those historical statements are not verification of the current branch.

Landing rounds 2 to 6 refined the statistics, clause band, FAQ, source marks, layout and source plates. The official source logos remain flagged for legal review. The Figma plugin and HTML pack were not resynchronised after round 6; see `09-landing-v2.md`.

Open decisions include public re-consent for quotes and stories, the generic permission example, the optional planned-treatment field and all legal-review copy, rendered headline and accessibility behaviour, name clearance and photography. Review and implementation planning remain separate actions.
