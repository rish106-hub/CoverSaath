What we will not do

Promise a claim
Only your insurer decides a claim. We show which clauses apply and what is still open.
Guess
A fact we cannot find stays marked Not confirmed until someone who knows confirms it.
Push a policy
Sometimes the right answer is to buy nothing yet. We will say so.
Show medical details
Not to anyone, unless that person chose to share them.
Replace your insurer
We are not your insurer, TPA or hospital desk. We help you ask them better questions.
